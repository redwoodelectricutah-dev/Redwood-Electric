About us

A Utah County shop with a name on the work.

Redwood Electric is James Hewitt. The company site is how you see the work. The configurator is how you start a scope. They stay connected, and they stay separate.

Who

Based near Elberta. On the road when the job is.

James runs the shop from Utah County. Residential electrical is the core. Low voltage grew up beside it: home theaters, UniFi cameras and networks, data racks, Loxone, and the wiring side of a basement that is turning into rooms.

We do not print a founding year on the wall until it is the real one. What we will say is simple: serving Utah County, licensed and insured, and willing to travel the Wasatch Front for the right project.

How a job goes

Three habits. No invented credentials.

Walk the house

A quote from the configurator is a starting picture. The real scope comes from seeing the panel, the paths, and the room the gear has to live in.

Plan it together

Low voltage is not an afterthought once the drywall is up. Power, network, and the theater or cameras are laid out as one job.

Leave it labeled

Breakers, patch cables, and racks should still make sense a year later. The photographs on this site are the standard, not a mood board.

Where
